Larmbo Mufasa

Slide every arrow off the board!

Level 1

Tap an arrow that has a clear path to the edge. A blocked arrow costs a heart.

How to play

  1. Every arrow points in one direction.
  2. Tap an arrow to slide it off the board.
  3. An arrow can only leave if nothing is in its way.
  4. Tapping a blocked arrow costs you a heart.
  5. You have 3 hearts. Lose them all and the level restarts.
  6. Clear every arrow to win the level.

Stuck? The Hint button shows an arrow you can safely tap.
